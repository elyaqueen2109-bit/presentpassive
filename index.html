✨ Present Simple Passive

🔹 FORM

am / is / are + V3 (past participle)

The mixture is cooked in an oven.

Different liquids are added to the paste.

Plastic bags aren't recycled.

How many banana skins are thrown away every day?

🔸 WHEN TO USE

We use the passive when the action is more important than who does it, or when we don't know who does it.

Bioplastics are made from starch. (We don't need to say who makes them.)

🔹 NEGATIVE & QUESTIONS

am / is / are + not + V3

The circuit board isn't designed correctly.

(Wh-) + am / is / are + subject + V3?

Is the banana skin dipped in a special liquid?

Where is starch found?

✅ Short forms accepted: isn't, aren't, 's, 're.

⚡ Tip: Regular past participles end in -ed (cooked, added, recycled). Irregular ones you need to remember (made, found, thrown, eaten, sold, etc.).
